## What failed before, what improved after, per class of mistake

"Before" is the old skill (SBI, no caps, three marker forms, no per-story skeleton) and the old
gate, which passed the real document. "After" names the layer that now catches the class: the
lint (countable, deterministic) or checklist section H (judgement, quoted-failure form), with the
line the red fixture trips.
